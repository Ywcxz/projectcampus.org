Project CAMPUS

Connecting All Members, Places, and University Services

A different approach to digital transformation at Visayas State University: a digital layer that connects its people, places, knowledge, and services. Proposed Overview

By Leo M. Subingsubing, a VSU alumnus. No VSU office has reviewed or endorsed it, and no field research has been run yet. Established Overview

What it proposes

The one product

A social/academic network

VSU's own online space for students, faculty, staff, and offices, where questions reach someone who can answer and answers stay findable. Proposed D-101 Ch 3

Long-term infrastructure

A campus intranet

What VSU makes lives on VSU's own network first, so campus services keep working when the internet link fails. Proposed Ch 2 A direction for VSU ICT, not a request. Established D-022
