Project CAMPUS

Connecting All Members, Places, and University Services

A proposal for a different approach to digital transformation at Visayas State University: a digital layer over the physical university that connects its people, places, knowledge, and services, designed around VSU's own culture, values, policies, geography, and people.

What it proposes

The one product

A social/academic network

VSU's own online space for its academic community: students, faculty, staff, and offices. Built first so that questions reach someone who can answer, and notices and answers stay findable and trustworthy; around that, organizations, events, and campus life. It is not built to hold attention. Its name is still a working name.

Proposed D-101 D-061 Q-58

A marketplace, housing near campus, and a delivery service are later modules. Each waits for its own evidence and is not part of the first version.

Established D-098

Long-term infrastructure

A campus intranet

What VSU makes would live on VSU's own network by default, with the wider internet as the outer ring. It is a direction offered to VSU ICT and the DIGITS program, not a request: CAMPUS does not ask VSU to build, fund, or host anything here.

Proposed the direction · Established that it is not a request Ch 2

Research only

A digital twin

A living model of the campus. Its first, smallest step would be finding places and knowing which office handles what. Everything beyond that step is research only.
