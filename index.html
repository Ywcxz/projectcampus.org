Project CAMPUS

Connecting All Members, Places, and University Services

A different approach to digital transformation at Visayas State University: a digital layer over the physical university that connects its people, places, knowledge, and services, designed around VSU's own culture, values, policies, geography, and people. Proposed Overview

Prepared by Leo M. Subingsubing, Visayas State University Main Campus alumnus

What it proposes

A network for VSU's people, and a campus intranet beneath it

One product, the social/academic network, with the campus intranet as its long-term infrastructure. Established D-098

The one product

A social/academic network

VSU's own online space for students, faculty, staff, and offices: academic at its core, with organizations, events, and campus life around it. Proposed D-101

  • Routing. A question reaches someone who can answer it.
  • Memory. A confirmed answer stays, dated and findable, for the next batch.
  • Trust. A notice shows who stands behind it, and whether it still holds.

Proposed Ch 3 D-063 D-070

Requests, course work, and grades stay in VSU's own systems, and the network helps people find them. It has no ads and no feed built to hold attention: it is judged by questions answered and answers reused, never by time spent. Proposed D-064 D-035 D-061

Long-term infrastructure

A campus intranet

What VSU makes would live on VSU's own network first, with the wider internet as the outer ring, so campus services keep working on campus when the internet link fails.

  1. Devicewhat each phone or laptop keeps
  2. CampusVSU's servers, fiber, and Wi-Fi
  3. VSU-widethe other campuses and offices
  4. Interneteverything outside

Proposed Ch 2

It builds on what VSU already runs: a fiber backbone, Wi-Fi across most of the Main Campus, and a typhoon-hardened data center. Established Ch 2

A direction offered to VSU ICT and the DIGITS program, not a request: CAMPUS asks VSU to build, fund, or host nothing here. Established D-022

Further out. A digital twin, a living model of the campus. Its first step would be finding places and knowing which office handles what; everything beyond that is research only. Proposed D-079 Ch 4

Later modules. A marketplace, housing near campus, and delivery. Each waits for its own evidence, and none is part of the network's first version. Established D-098

Where it stands

An early, independent proposal

Who
Led full-time by one person, a VSU Main Campus alumnus. There is no team, partner, or institutional collaboration. Established Overview
VSU
No VSU office has reviewed, endorsed, approved, funded, or adopted CAMPUS. Established Overview
Evidence
No field research has been run yet, so every problem CAMPUS addresses is still a hypothesis. Established Overview
First build
A small prototype of the network, on made-up data, which needs nothing from VSU. Established D-099
First approach
A concept note to the Office of the Vice President for Student Affairs and Services, with two separate asks: permission to run the student survey and interviews, and interest in CAMPUS. Established D-083
